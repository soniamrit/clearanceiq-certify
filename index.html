Step 2 of 3 · 32 CFR Part 170 Table 4
Asset Classification

Classify your in-scope assets per Table 4 of 32 CFR Part 170. This determines the full CMMC control scope for each asset type.

32 CFR §170.19(c)(2)(i) — Asset categories define which NIST SP 800-171 controls apply. CUI Assets and Security Protection Assets carry the full 110-control requirement.
● CUI Asset
Processes, stores, or transmits Controlled Unclassified Information directly. Full 110-control scope.
● Security Protection Asset (SPA)
Provides security functions for CUI Assets (firewalls, IAM, SIEM). Full 110-control scope.
● Contractor Risk Managed (CRMA)
Connected to CUI environment but not directly handling CUI. Contractor-defined controls.
● Specialized Asset
GFE, IoT, OT/ICS, test equipment. May have tailored requirements per asset type.
● Out of Scope
No connection to CUI environment. No CMMC requirements.
